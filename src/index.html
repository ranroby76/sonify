AI Musical Tools

Sonify's AI tools, each a small studio of its own. They work on this computer, on your song's clips, and each change is one Edit > Undo step.

To have Fanan AI do the work in your song, open the AI Assistant at the lower right.

Stem Splitter

A recording (a song, a mix) separated into its vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano and the rest, each on a new audio track under it, in time with it. In the same go, Sonify can turn each stem into MIDI and find the song's chords, key and tempo. It's all worked out on this computer; a whole song takes a few minutes.

Audio to MIDI

Sonify listens to a recording and writes what it plays as notes, in a MIDI clip on a new instrument track under it: a melody, chords or a beat, to play with another sound, edit, or take on to the MIDI Generators.

The recording

What it listens for

MIDI Generators

Sonify's MIDI tools on a clip: the built-in ones (arpeggios, chords, strums, echoes, grooves…) and Magenta's models, which write melodies, drum beats and harmony on this computer. Pick a tool, set it, choose the clip and Apply.

Tempo & Pitch

A recording put on the song's beat, moved up or down, turned around or levelled. The original is always kept: Back to the Original returns to it.

The recording

Tempo

Sync Tempo makes a stretched copy whose beats land on the song's; Follow stretches it again whenever the song's tempo changes. With its tempo left empty, Sonify listens for it first.

Pitch

Up or down by semitones (and hundredths), from the original's pitch; its length stays as it is.

Mastering Assistant

Sonify listens to the whole song through Main, then masters it with Sonify Mastering as Main's last insert: EQ, glue compression and a true-peak limiter that keeps peaks under -1 dBTP. The song plays silently, faster than real time.

Song Builder Blocks

A block is a copy of clips (a verse, a chorus). Click pads to put their blocks in the song's order, then build the song on the timeline.

Click a cell to launch it on its track, a block's name to launch all of it, ■ to stop a track. What you launch is written on the timeline as it plays (one Undo step).
